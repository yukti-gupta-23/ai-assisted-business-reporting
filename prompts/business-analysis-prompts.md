# Business Analysis Prompts

## 1. Excel Formula Validation

### Prompt

I have an excel table named Table1 with columns Employee, Department, Sales and Attendance. I need the total Sales for employees in the Sales department whose Attendance is at least 90%. Give me the Excel formula and explain each part. Do not assume any additional columns or data.

### Validation

The generated SUMIFS formula was tested directly in Excel and confirmed to be correct.

## 2. Management Summary

### Prompt

Act as an Operations Executive working at a company. I have meeting notes that are unorganized. Convert them into a professional management summary. Do not invent, assume, or add information that is not provided.

### Validation

The prompt was designed to prevent AI from inventing information and required the output to be checked against the original notes.

---

## 3. Meeting Notes Workflow

### Prompt

Act as an Operations Executive working at a company. I have unorganized meeting notes from a Weekly Operations Review. Convert them into a professional management summary with Executive Summary, Key Decisions, Action Items, Owner, Deadline, and Open Questions.

### Validation

The workflow requires missing owners or deadlines to be identified rather than assumed.
