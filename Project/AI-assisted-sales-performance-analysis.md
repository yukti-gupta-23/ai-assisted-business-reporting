# AI-Assisted Sales Performance Analysis

## Overview

This project demonstrates how Generative AI can assist with business analysis and management reporting using a sales performance dataset.

Microsoft Excel was used to clean, calculate, and analyze the data, while AI was used to assist with formula development, interpretation, management summaries, and recommendations. AI-generated outputs were independently verified against the Excel calculations and source data before being used.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to demonstrate an AI-assisted workflow for analyzing sales performance and converting verified business data into management-ready insights and recommendations.

## Dataset

The practice dataset contains:

- 10 employees
- 5 departments
- 4 regions
- Monthly sales
- Individual targets
- Attendance
- Customer complaints
- Orders
- Product information

## Key Analysis

The Excel analysis includes:

- Total sales and target achievement
- Employee-level sales performance
- Achievement percentage
- Performance status
- Attendance analysis
- Customer complaint analysis
- KPI calculations
- Management-focused business insights

## Key Findings

- Total monthly sales were ₹370,000 against a total target of ₹370,000.
- Overall achievement was 100%.
- Priya recorded the highest Sales-team sales at ₹91,000.
- Arjun recorded the lowest Sales-team sales at ₹54,000.
- Rahul and Arjun were below their individual targets.
- Average attendance was 92.1%.
- Four employees had attendance below 90%.
- Arjun had the lowest attendance and highest customer complaints among Sales employees.

## AI-Assisted Workflow

The project followed this workflow:

**Raw Data → Excel Analysis → AI Assistance → Human Verification → Business Insights → Management Report**

AI was used to assist with:

- Excel formula development
- Data interpretation
- Management questions
- Management summaries
- Management recommendations

## Human Verification

AI outputs were independently reviewed against the Excel calculations and source dataset.

The verification process checked for:

- Numerical accuracy
- Unsupported assumptions
- Invented information
- Missing important findings
- Unsupported causal claims

AI was used as an assistant rather than as the final decision-maker.

## Management Recommendations

The analysis recommends:

1. Reviewing the performance of employees below their individual targets.
2. Monitoring attendance patterns, particularly among employees below 90%.
3. Conducting further review of Arjun's sales, attendance, and customer complaints before drawing conclusions about relationships between these measures.
