# AI-Assisted Reporting Workflow

## Overview

This workflow demonstrates how AI can assist a repeatable weekly sales reporting process while keeping Excel analysis and human verification as key controls.

## Workflow

1. Receive and organize raw data in Excel.
2. Check and clean the dataset.
3. Perform initial analysis and calculate KPIs in Excel.
4. Use AI to assist with interpretation, management questions, summaries, and recommendations.
5. Independently verify AI outputs against Excel calculations and source data.
6. Create and format the management report using verified findings.
7. Conduct a final human quality check and send the verified report to management.

## Core Principle

AI assists the analyst but does not replace human judgment or make final business decisions.

## Quality Controls

- Verify numerical results in Excel.
- Check for invented information.
- Check for unsupported causal claims.
- Confirm important findings are not omitted.
- Do not send an AI-generated report to management without human review.
