# AI-Assisted Corporate Reporting Workflow

## Objective

This workflow demonstrates how Generative AI can assist with a corporate sales reporting process while keeping human verification and decision-making at the center of the workflow.

## Workflow 

### 1. Receive the Sales Data

Collect the latest sales data and check that the required information is available.

## 2. Analyze the Data in Excel

Use Excel formulas, functions, tables, and other analysis tools to understand the data and identify important metrics.

### 3. Create a Structured AI Prompt

Provide AI with the relevant context and clearly define the task, constraints, and expected output.

### 4. Review the AI Output 

Examine the AI response for incorrect, unsupported, or missing information.

### 5. Verify Against the Source
 
Compare important AI-generated findings with the original data and independent Excel analysis.

### 6. Make the Business Decision

Use the verified information, business context, and human judgement to support the final business decision.

## Core Principle

AI assists with analysis and reporting, but humans remain responsible for verification, judgement, and business decisions.
